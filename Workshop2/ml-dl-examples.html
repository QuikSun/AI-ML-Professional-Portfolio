Project notes

Purpose and audience: This artifact translates an academic comparison of machine learning and deep learning into examples that employers and business analysts can explore without reading model code or interpreting a benchmark.

Skills demonstrated: AI/ML conceptual understanding, research synthesis, explanation of model behavior, and adaptation of technical material for a professional audience.

Connection to the paper: The decision tree complements the paper’s discussion of conventional classifiers by making a classification path visible. It does not reproduce the paper’s SVM. The translation walkthrough illustrates contextual interpretation and sequential generation.

Limitations: The churn rules and 30-day prediction horizon are teaching assumptions with no validated predictive accuracy. The translation walkthrough supports two preset sentences and does not translate arbitrary text. These examples demonstrate inference concepts rather than model training or deployment.

AI assistance: ChatGPT helped adapt the explanations and generate the demonstration code. The code and presentation are AI-assisted; the examples do not establish original model-training results.
