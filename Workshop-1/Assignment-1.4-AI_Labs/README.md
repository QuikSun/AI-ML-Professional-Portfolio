**Generative AI Lab: Practical Applications, Research, and Design Thinking**

This assignment explores the practical applications of generative AI through hands-on exercises involving data analysis, prompt engineering, AI-assisted research, and design thinking.

Using ChatGPT, I analyzed synthetic survey participation data and compared multiple AI-generated responses to evaluate their consistency, accuracy, assumptions, and limitations. I also explored STORM AI for research on online survey participation and developed a conceptual AI-assisted recruitment solution using the five stages of design thinking.

The exercises demonstrated how generative AI can improve research efficiency, support analytical workflows, and assist with organizational problem-solving. They also reinforced the importance of validating AI-generated information, recognizing unsupported assumptions, addressing algorithmic bias, and maintaining human oversight.

**Tools:** ChatGPT, STORM AI  
**Skills Demonstrated:** Prompt engineering, data analysis, AI-assisted research, critical evaluation, design thinking, and responsible AI implementation.
