AI Timeline: The History and Evolution of Artificial Intelligence

This assignment explores the historical development of artificial intelligence through a visual timeline highlighting major milestones, technological breakthroughs, and developments in AI and machine learning.

The objective was to understand how AI has evolved from early theoretical concepts and rule-based systems to modern machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI applications. Creating the timeline helped me connect historical developments with current technologies and better understand how earlier innovations contributed to today's AI capabilities.

Skills Demonstrated: AI literacy, historical research, information synthesis, visual communication, and understanding of technological evolution.
