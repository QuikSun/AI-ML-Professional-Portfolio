About Me

Analytics, Research, and Emerging AI

I am a market research and data analytics professional with experience in survey research, data analysis, KPI reporting, and business intelligence. My background includes working with large datasets, identifying operational trends, developing analytical reports, and supporting data-driven business decisions.

My professional interests include applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to improve research operations, automate analytical workflows, and develop practical business solutions.

My experience combines market research, analytical reporting, and cross-functional coordination, allowing me to connect business questions with the data and processes needed to answer them. I am expanding this foundation through AI and machine learning studies, focusing on practical applications that support accurate analysis and informed human judgment.

Professional Background

Selected Experience & Contributions

Five areas of work spanning research, analytics, business intelligence, operations, and applied AI.

Research Design & End-to-End Delivery

Managed quantitative market research from survey planning and questionnaire design through fieldwork coordination, analysis, and client presentations. Worked across consumer-brand and healthcare research, including coordinating Taiwan fieldwork for an international brand-awareness study. This experience built a foundation in connecting business questions to reliable research methods and clearly communicated findings.

Panel Performance & Operational Decision Support

Analyzed survey feasibility, panel engagement, and operational KPIs to help research teams plan resources and deliver projects. Used SQL and cohort-based analysis to identify participation trends and inform operational improvements. In a separate analytics role, examined player behavior, segmentation, campaign timing, and conversion, translating findings into business requirements for developers.

Business Intelligence & Self-Service Reporting

Developed Power BI dashboards and self-service reporting tools to make performance indicators more accessible to research and operations teams. Also used Tableau and SQL to explore campaign and behavioral data. The emphasis was on turning recurring analytical questions into clearer, more usable reporting rather than one-off data extracts.

Cross-Functional Platform Migration & Process Improvement

Supported an eight-month transition from a third-party survey operations platform to an in-house system. Worked with research, operations, panel, and engineering teams to define essential data fields, workflows, and KPIs. Documented more than 20 pain points and identified seven useful features missing from early builds, helping technical teams understand operational needs and prioritize improvements.

Applied AI, Automation & Data Quality

Developed GPT-assisted workflows for selected survey feasibility and KPI reports, exploring ways to reduce repetitive reporting effort while retaining human review. Earlier work included collecting and validating voice datasets for Alexa-related machine learning development using established quality and annotation protocols. Together, these experiences inform a practical approach to AI: reliable inputs, clearly defined use cases, and accountable interpretation of outputs.
